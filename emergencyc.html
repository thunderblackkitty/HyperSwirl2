Emergency Contacts

Keep important numbers close. If you or someone nearby is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service.

Find a number
Help is one call away
KEEP THESE CLOSE

Emergency service numbers

Choose the service you need. On a phone, tap the number or call button to place a call.

Police

For immediate police assistance and urgent public safety incidents.

199 Call police

Fire service

For fires, rescue assistance, and other urgent fire-service emergencies.

191 Call fire service

Ambulance

For urgent medical emergencies. Give your location and describe what happened.

192 Call ambulance

Important: Emergency numbers and availability can vary by location or phone network. Confirm these contacts with local authorities and save nearby hospital and township emergency numbers too.

WHEN YOU CALL

Share clear, useful information

  • Give your township, street, and a nearby landmark.
  • Explain what happened and whether anyone is injured or in immediate danger.
  • Keep your phone available and follow the operator's instructions.
  • During a cyclone, follow official evacuation guidance and avoid flooded roads.

Make a family contact plan

Write down these numbers, choose an out-of-area family contact, and agree on a safe place to meet if you become separated.
