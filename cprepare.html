EMERGENCY & SAFETY

Be Prepared.
Stay Safe.

Cyclones can bring powerful winds, heavy rain, flooding, and storm surges. Preparing early helps protect you, your family, and your community.

Start preparing

Why prepare before a cyclone?

Warnings may leave little time to act. A household plan, essential supplies, and knowledge of safe routes can reduce confusion and help everyone make safer decisions. Follow local authorities and official weather updates.

YOUR SAFETY GUIDE

How to prepare

Take these steps before a cyclone approaches.

01

Stay informed

Keep track of official forecasts, warnings, and evacuation announcements.

  • Listen to trusted local news and official channels.
  • Charge your phone and keep a battery-powered radio if available.
  • Act promptly when authorities issue instructions.
02

Secure your home

Reduce hazards around your home before strong winds and rain arrive.

  • Bring loose objects indoors and secure doors and windows.
  • Move important belongings to a higher, dry place.
  • Stay away from damaged electrical wires.
03

Pack essential supplies

Keep an emergency kit somewhere easy to reach if you need to leave quickly.

  • Water, shelf-stable food, and a first-aid kit.
  • Medicines, flashlight, batteries, and power bank.
  • Important documents in a waterproof bag.
04

Make a family plan

Agree on how to reach each other and where to meet if you become separated.

  • Choose an out-of-area family contact.
  • Pick a safe meeting point and share it with everyone.
  • Write down emergency contacts.
05

Know where to go

Plan a safe route and learn where your nearest official shelter is located.

  • Plan an alternate route in case roads are blocked.
  • Arrange transport for anyone who may need help.
  • Never travel through floodwater.
06

Plan for everyone's needs

Consider children, older adults, people with disabilities, and pets.

  • Pack needed medicines and mobility aids.
  • Make arrangements for transport and assistance.
  • Include pet food and carriers where needed.
IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

Before, during, and after

Follow official instructions at every stage.

Before

  • Prepare your kit and charge essential devices.
  • Secure your home and keep important items dry.
  • Know your evacuation route and follow official warnings.

During

  • Stay in a sturdy shelter, away from windows.
  • Listen for official updates and instructions.
  • Avoid floodwater, coastlines, and downed power lines.
  • Do not go outside during a temporary lull in the storm.

After

  • Wait until authorities say it is safe to go outside.
  • Avoid damaged buildings and fallen wires.
  • Use safe drinking water and report hazards to authorities.
